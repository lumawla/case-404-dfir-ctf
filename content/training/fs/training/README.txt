PRACTICE FOLDER
===============

Nothing in here is part of the case, and nothing here is scored. The clock
hasn't started. Use this folder to get comfortable with the terminal.

The tasks are listed next to the terminal. The cheat sheet explains the
commands, with examples. Try `help` for the full list, and `help <command>`
for one command.

Things to know:
  - Paths: ~ is your home folder. `cd ..` goes up one level.
  - Tab completes file names. The up arrow brings back earlier commands.
  - Some files start with a dot. They are there, just not shown by default.
  - Answers look like FLAG{SOMETHING}.
