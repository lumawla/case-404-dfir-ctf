BLACKTHORN HALL - ACCESS CONTROL (ACS)

Staff badges open doors according to badges.csv. Readers marked with an exit
reader (STAFF-ENT, AV-STORE, OFFICE-2F, PROJ-EAST) also log EXIT when a badge
is used on the way out.

Guest wristbands (G, L and S series) are for entry, the photo booth and the
bar. They are not door credentials and open no doors.

The controller keeps a read-only mirror of every swipe. Staff can export the
log from the dashboard; each export gets an ID (ACS-EXP-nnnn) and can be
edited and saved again as a new export.
